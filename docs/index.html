Your health data should move carefully.

The rules we build by

Health data gets the strict path.

⌁

Correct meaning

VitalSync never labels Oura RMSSD as Apple Health SDNN. Measurements move only when their physiological meaning and units match.

◈

On-device control

Health records and sync history are kept on your device. HealthKit permissions remain separate and can be changed in iOS.

◇

No hidden audience

No analytics SDKs, ad networks, tracking pixels, or data brokers. The public website sets no advertising or analytics cookies.

Account security

VitalSync never asks for your Oura password.

Sign-in opens in Apple’s protected system authentication experience and loads Oura directly. Credentials and passkeys stay between you, the operating system, and Oura. VitalSync receives only the authorization result and stores resulting app tokens in the iPhone Keychain.

VitalSync is an independent application and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura Health Oy or Apple Inc.
