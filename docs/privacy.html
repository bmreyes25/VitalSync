Privacy policy

VitalSync is designed to move authorized health information without turning that information into a business model.

What VitalSync processes

When you connect Oura, VitalSync processes the Oura categories you authorize, such as daily summaries, heart rate, sleep, workouts, sessions, SpO₂, tags, stress, heart-health measurements, and ring configuration. The app also maintains local sync records needed to prevent duplicates and reconcile updates.

Oura sign-in

Oura authentication occurs on Oura’s website in Apple’s system web-authentication interface. VitalSync does not receive or store your Oura password, passkey, or verification code. A server-side token broker exchanges the authorization result because the Oura client secret must never be placed in the iPhone app. OAuth credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and are not stored by this website.

Apple Health

VitalSync requests separate HealthKit permission for each supported data type. You can allow or deny types individually. The app writes only data with a compatible physiological meaning and does not infer permission for types you deny. Oura RMSSD is never written as Apple Health SDNN.

Where data goes

Health records are stored locally on your device and, when you explicitly enable it, written to your Apple Health store. The public VitalSync website does not receive health data. VitalSync does not sell health information or use it for advertising, profiling, credit, insurance, or data-broker services.

Service providers

The OAuth token broker processes authorization codes and OAuth tokens only to connect the app to Oura. It is configured not to log request or response bodies. GitHub Pages hosts this informational website and may process ordinary web-server information under GitHub’s own policies. VitalSync adds no analytics or tracking scripts.

Retention and control

You can disconnect Oura from within VitalSync. The release version will also include controls to remove local imported data and sync history. Deleting samples previously written to Apple Health is managed separately through Apple Health and VitalSync reconciliation controls.

Security

VitalSync uses iOS Keychain protection for OAuth credentials, iOS Data Protection for local files, HTTPS for network traffic, redacted operational logs, explicit transfer confirmation, validated imports, and privacy shielding when the app leaves the foreground. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

Children and legal requests

VitalSync is not directed to children under 13. If applicable law requires a different minimum age where you live, that age applies. We may respond to valid legal requirements consistent with applicable law.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or deletion help, use the contact method on the support page.

VitalSync is independent of Oura and Apple.
