Privacy policy

VitalSync is designed to move authorized health information without turning that information into a business model.

What VitalSync processes

When you request an import, VitalSync reads up to seven days of authorized Oura heart rate, sleep HRV, daily sleep SpO₂ average, and readiness temperature deviation. It keeps these measurements and local sync records on your device to prevent duplicates and reconcile updates. The displayed HRV is Oura RMSSD, and temperature is a change from your personal baseline.

Oura sign-in

Oura authentication occurs on Oura’s website in Apple’s system web-authentication interface. VitalSync does not receive or store your Oura password, passkey, or verification code. A server-side token broker exchanges the authorization result because the Oura client secret must never be placed in the iPhone app. OAuth credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and are not stored by this website.

Apple Health

The current VitalSync build does not write data to Apple Health. Oura's own app can already share heart rate and other common measurements with Apple Health, so VitalSync avoids duplicating them. Future exports will be limited to missing data with a reviewed, equivalent HealthKit meaning and explicit permission. Oura RMSSD will never be written as Apple Health SDNN.

Where data goes

Imported health records are stored locally on your device. The current build does not write them to Apple Health. The public VitalSync website does not receive health data. VitalSync does not sell health information or use it for advertising, profiling, credit, insurance, or data-broker services.

Service providers

The OAuth token broker processes authorization codes and OAuth tokens only to connect the app to Oura. It is configured not to log request or response bodies. GitHub Pages hosts this informational website and may process ordinary web-server information under GitHub’s own policies. VitalSync adds no analytics or tracking scripts.

Retention and control

You can disconnect Oura and delete VitalSync’s local imported data and sync history in the app. The current build does not create Apple Health samples.

Security

VitalSync uses iOS Keychain protection for OAuth credentials, iOS Data Protection for local files, HTTPS for network traffic, redacted operational logs, and privacy shielding when the app leaves the foreground. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

Children and legal requests

VitalSync is not directed to children under 13. If applicable law requires a different minimum age where you live, that age applies. We may respond to valid legal requirements consistent with applicable law.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted here with a revised effective date. For privacy questions or deletion help, use the contact method on the support page.

VitalSync is independent of Oura and Apple.
