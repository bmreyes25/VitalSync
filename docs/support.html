Help and account control

VitalSync support

Get help without sharing passwords, OAuth tokens, or health-data exports.

Before contacting support

  • Never send your Oura password, passkey, client secret, authorization code, or token.
  • Never attach an Apple Health export or screenshot containing sensitive health details.
  • Describe the screen, general error, iOS version, and whether the issue affects Oura, Apple Health, or both.

Contact

Use the VitalSync repository to report a general problem or request help.

Open a support request

Do not include private health information, credentials, authorization codes, or tokens in a public issue.

Disconnecting Oura

Use VitalSync’s Privacy screen to disconnect the app. You can also revoke VitalSync from your Oura account. Disconnecting stops future access but does not automatically remove samples already written to Apple Health.

Apple Health permissions

Open the Health app, review Apps and Services, select VitalSync, and adjust individual permissions. iOS intentionally does not tell apps whether you denied read access to a type.

VitalSync is independent of Oura and Apple.
